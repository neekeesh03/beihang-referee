# Taylor & Francis reviewer guidance — operational distillate

Use these principles:

- unbiased review based on manuscript merit;
- confidentiality, conflicts and reviewer independence;
- constructive, comprehensive, evidenced report;
- verify references introduced in the review;
- call material similarity to editor attention;
- in revisions, assess whether changes actually resolve concerns;
- current T&F policy prohibits reviewers from uploading unpublished manuscripts to generative AI and from using AI to generate review reports; language polishing of a completed review may be permitted under declared conditions.
