# Elsevier reviewer guidance — operational distillate

Source family: Elsevier reviewer role, how-to-review, publishing ethics, and generative-AI policy pages.

Use these principles in the agent:

- evaluate quality, completeness, accuracy, methodological rigor, and journal-reader interest;
- remain constructive and specific;
- uphold confidentiality and reviewer accountability;
- avoid unsupported ethical accusations;
- distinguish essential revision from optional strengthening;
- in formal Elsevier review, obey the current AI/confidentiality restriction before manuscript processing.
