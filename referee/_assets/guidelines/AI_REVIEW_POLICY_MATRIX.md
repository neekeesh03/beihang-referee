# AI Use in Formal Peer Review — Caution Matrix

**Checked:** 2026-09-25. Re-verify live before a formal confidential review.

| Organization/publisher | Current operational stance for formal reviewers | Agent default |
|---|---|---|
| Elsevier | Reviewers must not upload submitted manuscript content into AI tools; limited supportive use such as language/structure help or background literature search may be allowed under policy conditions and human oversight | Block manuscript ingestion for formal review |
| Nature / Springer Nature | AI may support peer review, but human accountability/judgment cannot be delegated; manuscript content must not be uploaded to unsecured/public AI tools; disclosure and tool conditions apply | Verify journal/tool conditions; do not assume all AI is prohibited, but do not ingest into an unapproved/unsecured system |
| Wiley | Reviewers must not upload manuscript material into GenAI; limited review-language improvement may be permitted with disclosure, subject to journal policy | Block manuscript ingestion |
| Taylor & Francis | Reviewers must not upload unpublished manuscript content or use AI to generate/analyze the review; limited language polishing of a completed review may be allowed with disclosure | Block manuscript ingestion and scientific-review generation |
| JMIR | AI assistance requires accountability, confidentiality, and transparent disclosure; exact permitted use depends on current policy/tool handling | Proceed only if current confidentiality and disclosure conditions are satisfied |
| COPE | Ethical baseline emphasizes confidentiality, accountability, competence, objectivity, and authorized participation | Apply as baseline plus stricter journal/publisher rules |

Author-side self-review of one's own manuscript is a different use case from formal confidential peer review and is the primary intended use of this package.
