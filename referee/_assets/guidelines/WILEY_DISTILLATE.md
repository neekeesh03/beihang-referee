# Wiley reviewer guidance — operational distillate

Use these principles:

- assess originality, scientific significance, relevance, precision and completeness;
- test whether conclusions follow from evidence;
- examine whether similar or dissimilar published trends are acknowledged;
- evaluate replicability, reproducibility and robustness;
- identify major flaws before minor presentation issues;
- assess ethics and citation balance;
- separate journal aims/audience from scientific validity;
- formal Wiley peer review requires current confidentiality/AI-policy checks.
