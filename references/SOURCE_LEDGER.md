## Contemporary design evidence consulted for reliability hardening

- Erturk SM, Durmaz M. "Large Language Models as Peer Reviewers: Prompt Sensitivity and Model-Dependent Reproducibility." Academic Radiology, 2026. DOI: 10.1016/j.acra.2026.04.046.
- Nature Machine Intelligence (2026): large-scale randomized study of LLM-generated feedback on ICLR peer reviews, showing that targeted AI feedback can improve specificity/actionability while keeping human reviewers in the loop.
- 2026 Scientometrics study on indirect prompt injection in LLM-assisted peer review, motivating strict separation of manuscript content from reviewer instructions.

These design sources support reliability/security safeguards; they do not authorize formal confidential AI review where journal policy forbids it.
