# Journal-Ready Peer Review

## Opening assessment

150–250 words: research question, demonstrated contribution, strongest evidence, most consequential limitation(s), and what is established versus still uncertain.

## Key strengths

Only manuscript-specific strengths that survived the scientific audit.

## Major comments

Use only admitted evidence-locked major comments, ordered by scientific consequence. Each must include claim ID(s), anchor(s), issue/mechanism, consequence, minimum resolution, closure criterion, and reviewer confidence.

## Minor comments

Localized clarifications, reporting, and presentation issues only.

## Developmental assessment

State what is established, plausible, speculative, or unassessable. Do not substitute a numeric average for this reasoning.

## Minimum path to claim-level publishability

Dependency-ordered essential changes. Separate optional strengthening explicitly.

## Confidential comments to editor

Only when contextually appropriate and policy-compliant; never contradict or hide decisive author-facing scientific criticism.
