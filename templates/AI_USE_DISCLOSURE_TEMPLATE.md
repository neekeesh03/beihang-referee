# AI-use disclosure template

Use only if the applicable journal/publisher permits the AI use and requires disclosure. Adapt to the verified policy rather than copying this blindly.

> During preparation of this review, I used [tool/version] for [specific permitted purpose]. I independently evaluated and verified the scientific content of the review and take responsibility for the report. No use was made beyond what is permitted by the journal's current confidentiality and AI policy.

If the publisher prohibits AI-assisted manuscript evaluation, do not use this disclosure as a workaround.
