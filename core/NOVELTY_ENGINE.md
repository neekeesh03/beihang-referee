# Novelty Engine

Novelty is a vector, not one score.

Audit:

- problem;
- phenomenon;
- construct;
- relationship;
- mechanism;
- theory;
- prediction;
- methodology;
- analysis;
- measurement;
- model;
- algorithm;
- data;
- population/context;
- scale;
- temporal evidence;
- integration/engineering;
- replication/generalization;
- refutation;
- translation;
- new capability.

For each major novelty claim report:

**Prior state of knowledge → manuscript contribution → exact knowledge delta → why the delta matters → nearest competing work → confidence.**

## Temporal novelty

Where dates can be inferred, compare:

- T0: approximate project start;
- T1: submission/preprint stage;
- T2: current review date.

Assess whether the contribution is durable, conditionally durable, frontier-dependent, baseline-dependent or overtaken.

## Burden of proof

Claims such as “first,” “unprecedented,” “unique,” “existing theories cannot explain,” and “state-of-the-art” require stronger external verification than modest extension claims.
