# Springer Nature / Nature reviewer guidance — operational distillate

Use these principles:

- begin by understanding the research question, importance, methods, evidence, field advance and readership;
- ask whether methods are current and appropriate;
- examine reproducibility/detail of Methods, controls/comparators and validated outcomes;
- inspect figures/tables and statistical uncertainty;
- determine whether evidence supports conclusions;
- request new experiments only when necessary for the paper's current central claims;
- judge novelty and field influence using verified literature;
- keep author-facing scientific criticism visible rather than hiding it in editor-only comments;
- formal Nature/Springer Nature peer review requires strict confidentiality and current AI-policy checks.
