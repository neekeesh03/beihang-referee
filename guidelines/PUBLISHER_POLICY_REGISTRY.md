# Publisher / Ethics Policy Registry

**Policy snapshot checked:** 2026-09-25. Re-check live journal- and publisher-specific policy before any formal confidential review because policies evolve.

This file is an operational summary, not an official policy document. Journal-specific instructions can be stricter than publisher-wide guidance.

## Elsevier

Official source checked:

- https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals

Current operational summary:

- Submitted manuscripts are confidential.
- Reviewers must not upload a submitted manuscript or any part of it into an AI tool.
- Reviewers remain responsible for scientific assessment and judgment.
- AI may be used only in a supportive capacity that does not compromise manuscript confidentiality, such as improving the language/structure of a review the reviewer has written or background literature searching under the stated policy conditions.
- Disclosable AI use should be declared as required by the policy.

**Agent behavior:** formal confidential Elsevier review must not ingest manuscript content into the AI workflow.

## Springer Nature / Nature Portfolio

Official source checked:

- https://www.nature.com/nature/editorial-policies/ai

Current operational summary:

- Human accountability for scholarly evaluation is non-transferable.
- AI may support peer review but must not replace independent scholarly judgment.
- Reviewers must not upload manuscript content to unsecured or public AI tools.
- AI use should be disclosed for trust/transparency under the current framework.
- Springer Nature may have approved/private tools with different permitted uses; the exact journal/workflow conditions should be checked live.

**Agent behavior:** do not assume all AI assistance is categorically prohibited; verify the journal-specific and tool-security conditions. Never delegate the scientific judgment to AI in a formal review.

## Wiley

Official sources checked:

- https://authors.wiley.com/Reviewers/journal-reviewers/review-confidentiality-policy.html
- https://authors.wiley.com/ethics-guidelines/index.html

Current operational summary:

- Peer-review material is confidential.
- Reviewers must not upload manuscripts or manuscript parts, including figures/tables, into generative-AI tools.
- Limited AI use to improve the clarity/quality of a written review may be allowed with transparent declaration to the handling editor, subject to journal policy.
- Responsibility for the review remains with the human reviewer and must not be delegated to AI.

**Agent behavior:** formal confidential Wiley review must not ingest manuscript content into the AI workflow.

## Taylor & Francis

Official sources checked:

- https://taylorandfrancis.com/our-policies/ai-policy/
- https://editorresources.taylorandfrancis.com/reviewer-guidelines/

Current operational summary:

- Reviewers must not upload unpublished manuscript files, images, or information into generative-AI tools.
- Reviewers must not use AI tools to generate manuscript/proposal review reports or analyze/summarize submitted material for review creation.
- Generative AI may be used only to improve the language of a completed review under the stated conditions, with disclosure to the editor.

**Agent behavior:** formal confidential Taylor & Francis review must not ingest manuscript content or generate the scientific review.

## JMIR Publications

Official source checked:

- https://support.jmir.org/hc/en-us/articles/16470162812827-How-to-write-a-high-quality-peer-review

Current operational summary:

- Reviewer accountability remains with the human reviewer.
- Confidentiality of manuscript material must be preserved.
- AI-assisted peer-review use must be transparently disclosed under JMIR's current guidance.
- Commercial tools that reuse confidential content can breach confidentiality; exact permitted use should be checked against the current JMIR policy.

**Agent behavior:** verify current JMIR policy and tool conditions before any formal confidential review; do not expose manuscript content to a system that would compromise confidentiality.

## COPE

Official source:

- https://doi.org/10.24318/cope.2019.1.9

Operational principles:

- accept reviews only when competent and sufficiently unbiased;
- declare competing interests;
- read manuscript and ancillary material thoroughly;
- preserve confidentiality;
- report suspected ethical irregularities to the editor rather than independently investigating misconduct;
- be objective, constructive, specific, and professional;
- support consequential statements with evidence;
- keep recommendations consistent with the written review;
- do not use confidential editor comments for hidden denigration;
- request additional investigations only when essential to current claims.

## Enforcement rule

For formal confidential peer review:

1. check journal-specific reviewer instructions;
2. check current publisher policy;
3. check whether the actual AI environment/tool satisfies the permitted privacy/security conditions;
4. apply the stricter applicable rule;
5. record the source and date checked;
6. if policy forbids manuscript ingestion or AI-generated evaluation, stop manuscript-specific AI review rather than attempting a workaround.
