# JMIR reviewer guidance — operational distillate

Use these principles:

- concise overview of objectives/significance/strengths/limitations;
- audit literature coverage, methods/design/sample/statistics/bias and relevant reporting guideline;
- verify results align with research question and analyses;
- test discussion/conclusions for unsupported claims and overgeneralization;
- assess clarity/organization without reducing review to copyediting;
- identify ethics concerns constructively;
- provide specific recommendations and optional confidential editor comments;
- if AI assistance is permitted, preserve confidentiality, disclose use as required, and retain human accountability.
