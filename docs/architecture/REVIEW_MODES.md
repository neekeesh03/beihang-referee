# Review modes

`initial` performs a first-pass scientific review. `revision` compares an earlier and revised manuscript and is designed to reassess closure rather than merely detect textual change. `rebuttal` audits reviewer-comment traceability, author responses, and revised evidence. `meta_review` keeps reviewer positions separate before synthesizing agreement and disagreement. `editorial_screen` focuses on fatal scientific incompleteness before a full review. `reproducibility` focuses on data/code/environment/package evidence.

Review purpose is separate from depth. A revision review can therefore be standard, deep, or exhaustive.
