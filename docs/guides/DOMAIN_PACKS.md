# Domain specialist packs

The platform adds specialist review packs on top of the general methods/statistics/literature kernel. A domain pack never replaces the general reviewers; it adds field-specific failure modes and measurement conventions.

Current packs cover electrochemistry/fuel cells, materials characterization, environmental/process engineering, microbiology/biofilms/omics, energy/LCA/TEA, computational science, ML-for-science, and experimental metrology/analytical measurement.

For microbial fuel-cell work, the electrochemistry pack explicitly checks cell/electrode configuration, current and power normalization, polarization protocol, internal resistance, charge and coulombic balances, reactor loading/HRT, COD or substrate removal versus energy recovery, analytical quantification, electroactive-biofilm attribution, and scale-up claims. Comparisons across studies must be conditioned on compatible electrode area definitions, reactor geometry, substrate/loading, temperature, cathode chemistry and operating protocol.
