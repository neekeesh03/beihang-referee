# Reporting-guideline routing

The bundled reporting profiles are routing aids only. They contain broad trigger terms and presence/absence signals for families such as randomized trials, observational studies, systematic reviews, diagnostic studies, prediction models, economic evaluations, animal studies, qualitative studies, routinely collected health data, and ML/imaging work.

The runtime never labels a manuscript formally compliant from these heuristics. Current authoritative checklists must be consulted for a real compliance determination.
